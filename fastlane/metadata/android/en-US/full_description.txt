LunaDial is a free and open-source clock that turns a spare phone or tablet into an easy-to-read desk clock.

Features:

- Digital and analog faces, with a calendar beside the analog dial
- 12-hour or 24-hour time, optional seconds, second hand and leading zero
- Light, dark or system appearance, with system, Material You or custom accent colours
- Night display that is always on, follows a schedule, or follows the system dark theme
- Subtle periodic movement during night display to reduce OLED burn-in
- Keeps the screen awake and hides the system bars for desk use
- English and Simplified Chinese

LunaDial needs no network access, asks for no permissions and collects no usage data. All settings stay on the device.

It is a native Android app written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, so a single APK of about 1.8 MB covers every device running Android 7.0 or newer.
