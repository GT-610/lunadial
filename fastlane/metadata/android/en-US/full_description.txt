LunaDial is a free and open-source clock that turns spare devices into elegant, customizable desk clocks.

Features:

- Material 3 design
- Very low resource use
- Digital and analog clocks with an optional calendar
- 12-hour or 24-hour time
- Optional seconds, second hand, and leading zero
- Light, dark, or system theme, with a selectable accent color
- Night display mode that can be enabled manually, scheduled, or matched to the system theme
- Subtle periodic movement to help reduce OLED burn-in during long-running night display
- Always-on display support and immersive mode on Android

LunaDial needs no network access and collects no usage data. All settings are stored locally.
